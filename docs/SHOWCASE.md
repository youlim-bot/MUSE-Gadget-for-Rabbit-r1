# V1.5 showcase and Discord kit

Real native Android views captured on a Rabbit r1 at 480×640. The separate offline demo has no Internet, location, microphone or camera permission and cannot schedule alarms. Conversations, photo answers, weather, area names, battery values and reminders are fictional fixtures. The avatar is the original upstream Muse character. These screenshots do not demonstrate live AI output or real weather measurements.
