**MUSE Gadget for Rabbit r1 — V2.0**

I installed LineageOS and Muse on my Rabbit r1, then kept customizing it. V2.0 adds a virtual-pet mode that grows alongside the assistant!

🥚 Start with an animated egg and raise a companion over days. Your care influences its personality and adult form, with no visible hatch countdown.
🍓 Six foods, three mini-games, sleep, cleaning and medicine. Neglect has consequences, too.
🎵 Choose local background music, let it change with growth, and enjoy little birdlike sound effects.
💬 Keep talking to Muse or giving it tasks from inside the pet room.
⚙️ A new Settings menu, tidier controls, Wi-Fi access and clearer current/peak UV information.

It also includes Korean/Japanese/English UI and interpretation, photo questions and text translation, keyboard chat, optional ElevenLabs speech, local alarms/timers/reminders, memos/checklists and a charging weather clock.

The pet itself works offline; Muse replies and cloud speech still need a working connection.

Code, LineageOS installation/recovery notes and feature guides in English, Korean and Japanese:
https://github.com/youlim-bot/MUSE-Gadget-for-Rabbit-r1

Images: real Android UI captured in an offline emulator, using fictional data and simulated pet ages—not live AI replies. The custom dolphin was generated with Muse without a reference image.

Based on Cameron Pak’s muse-r1. This is my unofficial, non-commercial community customization—not an official Rabbit, Meta or Bandai product. Feedback welcome!
